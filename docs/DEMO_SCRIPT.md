# Three-minute sample demo

1. **0:00–0:25:** Open the Admin login, accept terms and enter. Show 650 classrooms, 13 blocks, today’s timetable and sample requests.
2. **0:25–0:50:** Open Campus Map. Choose C, tap a room, show Available / Not available and the reason. The map is a labelled schematic until the actual college image is attached.
3. **0:50–1:20:** Review the sample cancellation, add a decision reason, approve with makeup required. Open Rescheduling and confirm one ranked slot. Check the date-specific room status and notifications.
4. **1:20–1:50:** Approve the sample rep permission. Switch to Class Rep, open Change Classroom, choose the approved session and a suitable room. Saving consumes permission while the fixed weekly timetable stays unchanged.
5. **1:50–2:25:** Switch to Club Member. Open My Bookings, download the sample Cyber Lock HOD letter, and upload a sample signed PDF/image.
6. **2:25–2:50:** Switch to Admin. Review the signed letter and recheck/approve the booking. Show the room is unavailable for the approved period.
7. **2:50–3:00:** Show the audit log, reports and mobile-friendly navigation. Explain that the demo records are browser-persisted; Supabase production workflows and WhatsApp are not connected yet.
